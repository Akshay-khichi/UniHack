SLIDE 3: BRIEF ABOUT YOUR SOLUTION
- Solution Summary: SpecTrace is an automated product content enrichment engine designed for industrial distributors. It bridges the gap between messy, incomplete supplier feeds and search-ready e-commerce catalogs.
- Core Capabilities:
  1. Ingestion & Pre-processing: Cleans unstandardized distributor inputs, resolves placeholder brand tags, and normalizes cryptic abbreviations.
  2. 3-Tier Taxonomy & 5-Tier Copy Engine: Automatically maps products to standard Department > Class > Fine taxonomy hierarchies while generating ERP and e-commerce descriptions (Invoice <=40 chars ALL CAPS, Mobile 60-80 chars, Short, Long, and Marketing copy).
  3. 252-Column Enterprise Compliance: Populates all 252 canonical delivery headers with zero schema deviations, verified across 1,000 catalog items with 100% completion in both CSV and XLSX formats.
